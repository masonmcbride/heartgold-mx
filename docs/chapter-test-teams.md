# Curated chapter feature teams

These fixtures are separate from continuous progression. They grant no badges,
items, event flags or acquisitions to acceptance saves. Levels are chapter exit
scales; evolve/acquire through gameplay in the continuous route. Fixture moves
below are the original species’ last four distinct level-up moves at that level,
read from `wotbl.narc`; they are contracts, not evidence that later chapters run.
Mamoswine assumes the Blackthorn move-reminder evolution in this separate fixture.
The Violet composition matches the existing Falkner engine feature scenario.
